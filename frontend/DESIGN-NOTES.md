# Web design references

## Direction

The homepage introduces the local shopping flow with an editorial store photograph. The referral offer leads with the merchant, product, and API-supplied price, then guides the shopper to the discount pass. The cashback page gives the available balance the strongest emphasis and keeps the milestone and purchase history easy to scan.

Use burnt orange `#B54718` for primary actions, Dusty Mauve Pink `#B78391` for the brand accent, and Deep Mauve Charcoal `#503C44` for text and dark surfaces. Use IBM Plex Sans Arabic throughout. Set body text in regular weight, actions in semibold, and headings and balances in bold. Keep the documented API and Arabic RTL flow.
